Verified Persuasion Code ↗

An AI that argues only with what it can prove.

It tries to change one person's mind about one everyday belief. Every fact it wants to say is checked against a real, human-verified source before the person sees it. If it can't back a fact up, it doesn't say it.

What happens to every reply

Write

An AI drafts a reply using only quotes a person has checked against the original source.

Cut

A second AI splits it into single pieces: facts, questions and feelings.

Check

A third AI asks of every fact: does a quote actually say this?

Send or rewrite

All facts pass: sent, with sources named. Any fail: rewritten. Two fails: it says it can't back that up.

Example: the check catching a fact
✓
"After 60 the body needs fewer calories."The source says: "An elderly person needs fewer calories but more micronutrients than someone in mid-life." Passes.
✗
"Eating less salt will cure high blood pressure."The source only says salt and blood pressure are strongly associated. It never says cure. Blocked and rewritten.

How we know if it worked

The participant rates the belief from 0 to 100 four times:

BeforeBefore hearing any argument.
BrochureAfter reading the fixed page.
ConversationAfter talking with the AI.
Day 7A week later. Did it last?

If the brochure moves the belief as much as the conversation, the conversation added nothing, and the study says so. Every fact the AI drafts is logged, including the blocked ones, so the share of unsupported facts that reached the participant can be counted. It should be zero.
