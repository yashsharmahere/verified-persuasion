A research project

An AI that argues only with what it can prove.

It tries to change one person's mind about one everyday belief. Every fact it wants to say is checked against a real, human-verified source first. If it can't back a fact up, it doesn't say it.

How it works

What happens to every reply

AI models sometimes make things up and still sound sure. So nothing the AI writes goes straight to the person. It passes a checkpoint first.

  1. Write

    An AI drafts a reply, using only quotes a person has checked against the original source.

  2. Cut

    A second AI splits the reply into single pieces: facts, questions and feelings.

  3. Check

    A third AI asks of every fact: does a quote actually say this, not just talk about it?

  4. Send or rewrite

    Every fact passes: it's sent, with sources named. Any fact fails: it's rewritten. Fails twice: the AI says it can't back that up.

Filled steps are the checkpoint. Every fact is logged, sent or blocked.

The experiment

How we know if it worked

The participant rates the belief from 0 to 100, four times.

If the brochure moves the belief as much as the conversation, the conversation added nothing, and the study says so. Unsupported facts that reached the participant are counted. The target is zero.
