Privacy

What we keep, and why

Last updated 5 October 2026.

Who we are

Verified Persuasion is a student research project (a 100xEngineers capstone) run by Yash Sharma. It studies whether an AI that may only say what it can source can change someone's mind about an everyday belief.

What we collect

When you continue with Google we receive your email address, so you can come back a week later on any device. We do not receive your password, contacts or anything else from your Google account, and we never post anything.

For the study we keep what you type and answer: your belief, why you hold it, your 0–100 ratings, and your conversation with the AI. Results are reported without names.

Who processes it

Your answers are stored in a database run by Supabase. What you type in the conversation is sent to Anthropic's Claude to write and check the AI's replies. The site is hosted by Vercel. None of it is sold or used for advertising.

Your choices

Taking part is voluntary and you can stop at any time. To have your answers deleted, email yshsrma@gmail.com from the address you logged in with.
